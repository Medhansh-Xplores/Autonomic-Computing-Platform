AUTONOMIC COMPUTING
PLATFORM (ACP)

Patterned on the human body's self healing autonomic nervous system

Our innovative solution, the "Autonomic Computing Platform (ACP)™" enables rapid buildup, teardown and deployment of scalable and self-healing infrastructure, containerized solutions, and applications across the multi and hybrid clouds; - in a repeatable and turnkey manner using Cloud, AI, and Automation. The platform leverages a plug-n-play architecture, Infrastructure and Security as Code, Kubernetes, AIOps and Opensource technologies to enable a self-managing computing model for high availability, resilience and, full-stack observability across distributed and hybrid cloud architectures for digital enterprises.

The infrastructure and systems deployed using the platform (ACP™), are highly Interoperable, Autonomic and Distributed Intelligent Systems that continuously learn, evolve, and optimize automatically for increased efficiency through situational awareness and contextual adaptation. Thus enabling "Autonomic Computing" through self-service features like Self-Configure, Self-Heal, Self-Optimize and Self-Protect the system. This self-managing computing model is named after, and patterned on, the human body's autonomic nervous system that regulates body systems without conscious input from the individual.

The benefits of this innovative computing model™ include (1) highly calibrated levels of intelligence, security, and autonomy for the system, (2) the system re-engineers to the desired state under issues or attack, (3) overall reduction in operations and maintenance costs, and (4) improvement in agility, security, and resilience of systems.
